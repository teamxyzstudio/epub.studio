# Surface brief: landing program (src/pages/[...landing].astro)

Scope: the four SEO landing types — audience, comparison, pillar, variant —
generated from src/content/landing/*.md via .claude/skills/epub-landing-writer.
Visitor mode: Persuade with an embedded Operate moment (pillar: Read with
Persuade edges).

Audience & job: searchers arriving with a job (audience), a competitor in mind
(comparison), a research question (pillar), or a situational modifier
(variant). Action: complete the conversion on the page via the embedded live
ToolRunner; secondary, click into Style Studio and guides.

Proof & constraints: the working tool IS the proof — no fabricated logos,
counts, testimonials ever. Comparison claims fetch-verified + dated with a
mandatory theirWins list. Distinct-intent test gates variant URLs; same-intent
modifiers enrich tool pages instead. Flat URLs registered in
content-ops/keyword-map.csv. WCAG 2.1 AA; single 760px breakpoint.

Direction (committed): "workbench, not brochure" — serif job-naming h1, live
drop zone in the first viewport on a Card panel, tool-page skeleton (920px;
pillar uses the article shell + TOC rail). Memorable moment: the comparison
table's "Where {competitor} is better" card — honesty as the conversion device.
Accent action is stage-gated: Pink Ink link at rest, the pink CTA arrives with
a chosen file; never more than one accent action visible. Recorded carve-out:
the pillar ships without an embedded runner (Read mode per confirmed brief §3);
the validator carries its CTA. Text-level accent is always Pink Ink #C41E4F
(WCAG AA); Electric Pink is reserved for fills, bars and borders.

Unresolved: topic-spreadsheet column mapping (documented in the skill);
which competitors beyond Calibre earn pages; routine amendment for landing
generation (deliberately after seed approval).
