    Choose your EPUB

  3. 03
    Convert and download
    Press Convert to PDF, then Download PDF. Your file is deleted after an hour.

EPUB vs PDF — what changes

EPUB — REFLOWABLE

Text reflows to fit any screen. Readers control text size, font and theme. Page numbers don’t exist — position is a percentage.

PDF — FIXED

Every page is frozen at the size you choose here. Perfect for printing and annotation; text no longer adapts to the screen.
